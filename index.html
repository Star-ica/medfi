💰 Deposit to Pool

Every deposit earns 10 MFT. From a 5-week streak onward, each deposit earns a 5 MFT bonus. Your savings set your max loan.

📅 Weekly Streak

Deposit at least once each calendar week to keep your streak.

📤 Withdraw Savings

You must have no active loans to withdraw. Withdrawals can fail while pool funds are lent out.

🏥 Request a Health Loan

Max loan is 3× your savings. A registered MedFi member must be your guarantor and stake half the loan to activate it. Fee: 2% flat. Term: 30 days from activation.

✅ Loans Waiting for You as Guarantor

Staking sends half the loan amount to the pool and disburses the loan to the borrower. You get your stake back when they repay. If they default, you lose it.
